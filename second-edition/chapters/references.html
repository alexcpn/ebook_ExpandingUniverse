Sources of the first edition

  2. Robert C. Smith, Observational Astrophysics. Cambridge University Press, 1995.
  3. Robert Wagoner and Donald Goldsmith, Cosmic Horizons: Understanding the Universe. W. H. Freeman, 1983.

  5. Charles R. Cowley, An Introduction to Cosmochemistry. Cambridge University Press, 1995.
  6. Ian D. Lawrie, A Unified Grand Tour of Theoretical Physics. Adam Hilger, 1990.

Further reading

  1. Barbara Ryden, Introduction to Cosmology, 2nd ed. Cambridge University Press, 2016. A clear undergraduate text covering everything in this book with full derivations.
  2. Edward R. Harrison, Darkness at Night: A Riddle of the Universe. Harvard University Press, 1987. The history and resolution of Olbers' paradox.
  3. Steven Weinberg, The First Three Minutes, updated ed. Basic Books, 1993. The classic popular account of the hot Big Bang and nucleosynthesis.
  4. P. J. E. Peebles, Cosmology's Century. Princeton University Press, 2020. A history of modern cosmology by one of its makers.
