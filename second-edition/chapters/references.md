# References and Further Reading

## Sources of the first edition

1. Frank H. Shu, *The Physical Universe: An Introduction to Astronomy*. University Science Books, 1982.
2. Robert C. Smith, *Observational Astrophysics*. Cambridge University Press, 1995.
3. Robert Wagoner and Donald Goldsmith, *Cosmic Horizons: Understanding the Universe*. W. H. Freeman, 1983.
4. Dennis Overbye, *Lonely Hearts of the Cosmos*. HarperCollins, 1991.
5. Charles R. Cowley, *An Introduction to Cosmochemistry*. Cambridge University Press, 1995.
6. Ian D. Lawrie, *A Unified Grand Tour of Theoretical Physics*. Adam Hilger, 1990.

## Further reading

7. Barbara Ryden, *Introduction to Cosmology*, 2nd ed. Cambridge University Press, 2016. A clear undergraduate text covering everything in this book with full derivations.
8. Edward R. Harrison, *Darkness at Night: A Riddle of the Universe*. Harvard University Press, 1987. The history and resolution of Olbers' paradox.
9. Steven Weinberg, *The First Three Minutes*, updated ed. Basic Books, 1993. The classic popular account of the hot Big Bang and nucleosynthesis.
10. P. J. E. Peebles, *Cosmology's Century*. Princeton University Press, 2020. A history of modern cosmology by one of its makers.
