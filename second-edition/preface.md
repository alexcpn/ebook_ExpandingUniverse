# Preface

## Preface to the Second Edition

In 1998 I wrote a short report, as a physics undergraduate, on two discoveries that seemed to me the greatest of the century: that the Universe is expanding, and that it began in a hot, dense state. That same year, two teams of astronomers announced that the expansion is not slowing down, as everyone had expected, but speeding up. The report does not mention it.

This edition keeps the shape and the argument of the original: the climb up the distance ladder, Hubble's law, and the three pillars of evidence for the Big Bang. It corrects what was wrong, explains what was skipped, replaces the borrowed textbook figures with new diagrams, and ends with a new chapter on what nearly three decades of observation have added. The first edition remains available, unchanged, alongside this one.

## Preface to the First Edition (1998)

Amidst the boundless, infinite Universe, humanity and its world are but a mere speck of physical insignificance. But our imagination is infinite and boundless; and those greats of our race, who have put their imagination, intuition and deduction to harmonious use, have come up with amazing facts of our Universe and great theories to account for the observations.

In the following account two of the greatest scientific discoveries of our time, that is, the discovery of the Expanding Universe and the Big Bang theory of the origin of the Universe, are presented with an account of the observational and theoretical methods employed in the discovery.
