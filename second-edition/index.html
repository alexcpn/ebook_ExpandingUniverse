Introduction

The Expanding Universe & The Big Bang Model

Second Edition, 2026

Author: Alex C Punnen

Based on the author's B.Sc Physics Project Report of 1998

This is a revised edition of a project report written in 1998. The first edition is kept unchanged as a record of what was written then; this edition uses it as a base and adds:

  • New diagrams. Every figure has been redrawn from physics or from published, cited measurements. The scanned textbook figures of the original have been replaced. The figures are produced by a script (scripts/make_figures.py), so anyone can regenerate or check them.
  • Corrections. Errors in the original, from a wrong proportionality in the Cepheid law to a misdated quotation, are fixed. Each one is listed in Changes from the First Edition.
  • Fuller explanations where the original skipped a step, for example why the Big Bang produces about 25% helium.
  • A new Part III, describing what has been learned since 1998: the year the Universe was found to be accelerating.
